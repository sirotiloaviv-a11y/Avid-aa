### 0.1 Identity Locks (copy these exactly; every shot prompt below already contains them)

**WARDROBE LOCK**
> high-collared black military-cut coat with gold circuit-pattern embroidery and blue gem studs on the collar, black turtleneck underneath

### 0.2 Story Rule for the Crown
When he unmasks (Shot 2.06) the **mask detaches from the crown — the crown stays on his head.** From then on, his real face is always framed under the crown. This mirrors the poster (half mask / half face) and becomes the film's signature image.

### 0.3 Cast
| Character | Description |
|---|---|
| **THE AI KING** | The protagonist. Masked legend of the cyber underworld. Voice through the mask: deep, digitally layered. Real voice: calm, low, human. |
| **COMMANDER RHEA VANCE** | Leader of Task Force HALO. 40s, short silver-streaked black hair, scar across left eyebrow, black tactical armor. |
| **HALO OPERATORS** | SWAT in matte-grey armor, faceless helmets with white visor strips. |
| **Ø (THE MASK AI)** | The intelligence living inside the mask. Speaks only in the final minute. |

### 0.4 Generator Workflow (how to keep him looking like the reference)
1. **Image-to-video, always.** Build a still "first frame" for each shot (Midjourney/Flux/Ideogram + your face reference, or a Runway/Luma reference image), then animate it. Text-to-video alone will drift the face.
2. **Runway Gen-3 / Gen-4:** 10s max per clip → longer shots below are marked `[EXTEND]`; generate, then use *Extend* from the last frame. Use the reference photo of his face as the image input for every unmasked close-up.
3. **Luma Dream Machine:** best tool for **Shot 2.06 (the unmasking)** — use *Keyframes*: Start frame = masked poster image, End frame = unmasked face under crown.
4. **Sora:** handles up to 20s and complex blocking; use it for the long action takes (3.04, 3.07, 4.06). Use your own likeness only (Cameo / consent features) — the face reference is you.
5. Keep camera language in every prompt (lens, movement, lighting). Put the **subject first, camera second, lighting third, style last** — that ordering is what these models weight most.
6. **Color pipeline:** Deep navy + gold + teal neon. Grade everything to this palette in post (DaVinci: teal-orange LUT, crush blacks, lift golds).

### 0.5 Universal Style Suffix (already appended in every prompt)
> cinematic Hollywood blockbuster, ultra-photorealistic 8k, anamorphic lens, shallow depth of field, volumetric haze, realistic skin texture, film grain, deep navy and gold color grade
