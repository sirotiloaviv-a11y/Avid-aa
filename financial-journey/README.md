# מ־18 ל־30 — Financial journey animation

A 39-second 3D animation (Hebrew) of a young Israeli's money from age 18 to 30:
army service, the big post-army trip, university, and a first tech job, with a
floating graph of net worth and bank balance drawn in real time behind him.

- `index.html` — the animation. Self-contained: a small flat-shaded 3D renderer
  on Canvas 2D, no libraries. Open it in a browser; the chapter bar seeks, space
  pauses. Append `#record` to hide the controls for screen capture.
- `financial-journey.webm` — a 1280×720 recording of one full run.

All figures are illustrative.
